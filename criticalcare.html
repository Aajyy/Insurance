Comprehensive Critical Care Insurance for Life-Threatening Illnesses

Our Critical Care Insurance provides comprehensive financial protection against life-threatening illnesses and major medical conditions. From cancer and heart diseases to organ transplants and neurological disorders, we offer specialized coverage with lump sum payouts to help you focus on recovery without financial stress. Enjoy coverage for early, intermediate, and advanced stage critical illnesses, additional benefits for recurrence, and comprehensive support services for complete peace of mind during challenging times.

Critical Care Insurance Features

Our critical care insurance plans come with comprehensive features designed to provide complete financial protection and support during critical illnesses.

  • Illness Coverage
  • Payout Benefits
  • Stage Coverage

  • Claim Process
  • Customer Support

Comprehensive coverage for major critical illnesses including cancer (all types), heart attack, coronary artery bypass surgery, and kidney failure. Coverage for major organ transplants (heart, liver, kidney, lung, pancreas), stroke, multiple sclerosis, and motor neuron disease. Protection against major burns, coma, blindness, deafness, and paralysis. Coverage for advanced Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and other neurological disorders. Protection against bacterial meningitis, end-stage lung disease, and end-stage liver failure. Coverage for major head trauma, benign brain tumor, and other life-threatening conditions.

Additional coverage for rare diseases like muscular dystrophy, aplastic anemia, and primary pulmonary hypertension. Protection against loss of independent existence and terminal illness. Coverage for angioplasty and other major cardiovascular procedures. Protection against major autoimmune diseases like lupus and rheumatoid arthritis. Coverage for major surgeries including open-heart surgery and neurosurgery. Protection against major infectious diseases requiring intensive care treatment.

Lump sum payout upon diagnosis of covered critical illness, regardless of actual medical expenses. Full sum insured payment for major critical illnesses like cancer, heart attack, and organ failure. Partial payouts for early-stage critical illnesses allowing continued coverage. Additional payout for recurrence of same or different critical illness after recovery. Waiver of premium benefit where future premiums are waived upon critical illness diagnosis. Increasing cover option where sum insured increases annually to counter medical inflation.

Return of premium option where premiums are returned if no claim is made. Additional survival period benefit if the insured survives beyond specified period. Child critical illness cover as add-on benefit. Second opinion benefit covering consultation costs with specialists. Hospital cash benefit providing daily allowance during hospitalization. Transportation benefit covering ambulance and travel expenses for treatment.

Early stage coverage for minor critical conditions with partial payouts (10-25% of sum insured). Coverage for carcinoma-in-situ, early-stage prostate cancer, and early-stage thyroid cancer. Protection for minor heart conditions like coronary angioplasty. Coverage for early-stage neurological conditions and minor organ involvement. Intermediate stage coverage for moderate critical conditions with significant payouts (50-75% of sum insured). Advanced stage coverage for major critical illnesses with full sum insured payout.

Progressive coverage where policy continues after early-stage payout. Multiple claim option for different critical illnesses at different stages. No reduction in sum insured after early or intermediate stage payouts. Special waiting periods for different stages of critical illnesses. Coverage for pre-existing conditions after specified waiting periods. Special provisions for genetic testing and advanced diagnostic procedures.

Second medical opinion service from panel of specialist doctors worldwide. Medical case management assisting with treatment planning and coordination. Hospitalization assistance for admission to network hospitals. Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation services. Psychological counseling and mental health support services. Rehabilitation services including physiotherapy and occupational therapy.

Nutrition counseling and dietary planning services. Home nursing care coordination for post-treatment recovery. Medical equipment rental assistance for home care. Transportation services for medical appointments. Child care assistance during treatment period. Legal and financial advisory services for estate planning.

Simplified claim process with minimal documentation requirements. 24/7 claim registration through toll-free number and online portal. Quick claim settlement with most critical illness claims processed within 10-15 working days. Digital claim submission with medical reports upload facility. Dedicated critical illness claim specialists for personalized assistance. Direct communication with treating doctors for medical verification.

Emergency claim processing for life-threatening conditions. Home visit for claim documentation collection for bedridden patients. Medical board review for complex critical illness cases. Transparent claim status tracking with regular updates. Grievance redressal mechanism with senior management escalation. Rehabilitation support coordination after claim settlement.

24/7 customer support through dedicated critical care helpline. Multi-lingual support and special assistance for critically ill patients. Medical coordination services with hospitals and specialists. Emotional support and counseling services for patients and families. Policy renewal reminders with continued coverage assurance. Health awareness programs and preventive care guidance.

Digital policy management through mobile app and customer portal. Instant policy document access and critical illness certificates. Proactive health alerts and early detection reminders. Medical research updates on critical illness treatments. Family support services during treatment period. Community support groups and patient networks.
