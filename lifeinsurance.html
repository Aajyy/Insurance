Child education and marriage benefits with special riders for children's future planning. Waiver of premium benefit in case of disability or critical illness. Flexible premium payment options including yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, and monthly modes through ECS, auto-debit, or online payments. Premium amounts based on age, health condition, sum assured, and policy term with discounts for non-smokers and healthy individuals. Premium payment terms ranging from single pay to regular pay up to policy term. No medical examination required for certain age limits and sum assured amounts with simplified underwriting process.

Premium rebates for higher sum assured and online policy purchases. Loyalty additions and bonuses for policies maintained without lapses. Surrender value available after premium payment for specified period with paid-up value option. Revival period of 2-5 years for lapsed policies with medical examination if required. Special premium rates for women and preferred lives with healthy lifestyle and family discounts available.

Death benefit paid to nominees in case of policyholder's demise during policy term. Maturity benefit paid on survival to policy term end for endowment and money-back plans. Bonus additions declared annually for participating policies. Guaranteed additions for specific policy types. Surrender value available after premium payment for specified period. Loan facility against policy for emergency financial needs with competitive interest rates.

Critical illness rider providing lump sum payout on diagnosis of specified illnesses. Accidental death and disability rider for enhanced protection. Waiver of premium rider exempting future premiums in case of disability. Income benefit rider providing regular income to family. Hospital cash benefit for daily expenses during hospitalization. Child benefit riders for education and marriage expenses with guaranteed payout options.

Tax exemption on bonuses and loyalty additions accrued during policy term. Tax benefits available for NRIs subject to DTAA provisions. Form 16A provided for tax deduction at source on maturity proceeds if applicable with proper documentation support.
